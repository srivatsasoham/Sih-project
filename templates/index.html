Slide 02 • Built for Real-World Service Failures

Edge-Case Resilience Matrix

Traditional platforms break when network fails, workers no-show, or disputes happen. SahiDeal solves real-world failures natively.

No-Show Worker

Auto-Reassign + Trust Penalty

If a worker fails to confirm arrival within 15 mins, platform issues automated trust penalty and re-routes to nearest pro within 1.2km.

Low Connectivity

Twilio IVR & SMS Fallback

Works 100% offline for senior citizens and low-network areas via automated local language voice calls and SMS OTPs.

Service Dispute

Escrow + Citizen Verification

Escrow held safely in neutral lock. 3 random peer co-op members verify before/after photos and vote on resolution.
