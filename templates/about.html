SLIDE 02

A Fair and Trusted Marketplace for Local Services

The model keeps platform economics simple while putting governance and upside in workers' hands.

01

Trusted Local Services

Customers can find nearby workers using verified profiles, badges, ratings and community references. Hyperlocal vouched ratings + verified community badges.

02

Workers Ownership

Eligible and verified workers can take part in cooperative ownership and self-governed decision-making (1 Member = 1 Vote in Co-op Council).

03

Fair Earnings (8% Fee)

8% platform fee. Lower take-rate designed to maximize worker income retention while funding shared health safety nets and dividends.

Built for Real-World Service Failures

No-show worker:

Auto-reassign algorithm + trust penalty recorded on ledger.

Service dispute:

Escrow neutral lock + peer citizen tribunal verification.

First-time worker:

PMKVY RPL skill credentialing + community peer vouching.

Low connectivity:

Twilio automated IVR voice booking / SMS fallback.

Women safety:

Women-provider filter + verified identity + 15m SOS.

Review fraud:

Verified completion signals (Start/End OTP + Work Photos).

SLIDE 04

Feasible Now. Designed to Scale Responsibly.

10–12 Week MVP
  • Core booking, matching, escrow & verification workflow.
  • Digital worker onboarding + Aadhaar eKYC verification.
  • Worker supply partnerships via PMKVY, ITIs & SHGs.
  • Co-op structure aligned to the statutory Multi-State Co-op basis.

Key Risks → Mitigation Strategy

Low worker supply: ITIs + PMKVY Skill Hubs + Women Self-Help Groups (SHGs)
New-platform trust: Community references + verified work photo proof + transparent ratings
Payment disputes: Escrow neutral lock + start/end photo proof + completion OTP
Worker misclassification: Statutory Multi-State Cooperative Society legal incorporation
